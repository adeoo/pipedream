This workbook has three parts. Part 1 is a classification drill. Part 2 is an error hunt. Part 3 is writing practice, with model answers.

Notes about the format. The answer options are short. The length of an option does not show the correct answer. Some cases are true border cases. For these, "Grey zone" is the correct choice. One drill has two defensible answers, and its feedback says so. There are no grades and no timers. You can try again after each wrong answer.

Part 3 · Your own analysis

This part has no shortcuts. Write your answer first. Then compare it with the model answer. Only you see your text.

Exercise 1 · Two accounts of one fact

In fifteen years, "entrepreneur" became the dream job of young people in the urban peripheries. The dream of their parents was a carteira assinada. Write the two accounts: first the idealist account, then the materialist account.

One idealist model: a new culture arrived — self-help books, business influencers, prosperity preaching. The culture changed the values of young people. Independence replaced security. New minds made new dreams.

One materialist model: the dream of the carteira assinada died with the formal jobs. The factories left. Apps and outsourcing replaced contracts. "Entrepreneur" became the name of what remained: sales on Instagram, driving, delivery. The word changes necessity into ambition. It makes each unemployed person a failed business. The influencers did not create the dream. They gave a good vocabulary to conditions already in place. Day 4 bonus question: who wins when precarity has the name of freedom?

Test your version with one question: does your materialist account explain the timing? Why this generation, and not the parents? The idealist account fails that test.

Exercise 2 · Predict, then check against history

Sudan, 2019. The bread price triples. Protests continue for months. A professionals' association leads them. Workers, students and merchants join. The dictator of thirty years falls. The army makes a "temporary" power-sharing agreement with civilians. Use Day 5 strictly. Write your prediction for the next three years. Then check.

Write the prediction before you look. That is the discipline.

What happened: October 2021. The rotation of the council to civilian control comes near. The generals make a coup. They dissolve the civilian government. A large part of the business class accepts "stability". The resistance committees of the neighbourhoods — the working-class base of the movement — continue almost alone. In 2023, the two armed factions start a war against each other.

The 1848 pattern, if your prediction found it: the alliance holds while the demand is liberty. The removal of the dictator costs the owners nothing. The alliance breaks when the question becomes property and power. The armed guards of order play the part of Cavaignac. The owners select them.

An honest note: your prediction can be different and still be good analysis. Name the Sudanese condition that the 1848 template misses — the army's own companies, or the foreign backers. That is analysis, not failure. A template that fits everything explains nothing.

Exercise 3 · Make the concepts work on each other

Day 2 says: arrangements break from internal contradiction. Day 5 shows the February alliance break in June. Write the contradiction of the February alliance in the Day 2 form: "It needed ___, and it needed ___, and it could not have both."

One model: the alliance needed the numbers of the workers on the barricades, to defeat the kings. It also needed to protect property against those same numbers, to keep its bourgeois wing. Each step that armed the workers for February prepared the conflict of June. The victory of the alliance ripened its own split. The breakup was not a betrayal. Better persons could not prevent it. It was the contradiction in resolution. That is the Day 2 claim about how arrangements end.

Your answer can be different. Test it: does it name two opposed necessities inside the alliance? Not an outside enemy. Not bad faith. If yes, your answer does the work. You used one tool of week 1 to explain the result of another. At that moment, a set of lessons becomes a method.

Workbook: closed ✔

What each part trains. Part 1: see the relation through each costume — titles, legal wrappers, salaries, sympathy. Part 2: quality control on analysis itself. The method's worst enemies wear its uniform: the villain, the spark, the conspiracy. Part 3: production. Only production shows that you can use the method outside a quiz. If your Sudan prediction found the pattern before the reveal, the week did its work.
