# Daily Marxism — Program

A daily email course on Marxism and communism for Moussa (moussaadel97@gmail.com),
taught from within the tradition — sympathetic, committed, rigorous. Personalized:
Moussa is Lebanese and Paraguayan; the Palestinian struggle and Latin America
(Cuba, Brazil, Paraguay) are central threads, not side notes.

## Delivery
- One email per day at **06:00 America/Sao_Paulo (UTC-3)** → schedule with Resend `scheduledAt` in ISO form, e.g. `2026-08-29T06:00:00-03:00`.
- From: `Daily Marxism <onboarding@resend.dev>` → To: `moussaadel97@gmail.com`.
- Subject format: `☭ Day N — <Title>`.
- Batches are written weekly (Thursday), covering Friday→Thursday.

## Email format (~5 minute read, ±600 words)
Every email, in order:
1. **📜 Where we are in history** — 2–4 sentences situating the lesson in its historical moment. Always first.
2. **The lesson** — one core concept, explained plainly and vividly, in a committed voice. Teach the idea as its thinkers meant it; don't hedge it against liberalism. Connect forward/backward to neighboring lessons.
3. **📖 From the source** — a short quote from an original text. Where natural, give key terms/quotes in Arabic, Spanish, or Portuguese with translation (e.g. صراع الطبقات, plusvalía, os condenados da terra).
4. **🤔 Reflection** — 1–2 questions inviting the reader to apply the concept to Lebanon, Palestine, Paraguay, Brazil, or daily life.
5. Footer: `Week W, Day N of the program · Daily Marxism`.

HTML style: single column, max-width 600px, Georgia/serif body, context section in a light tinted box, quote as a styled blockquote with left border. Always include a plain-text version.

## Voice
- Committed and warm, like a well-read comrade writing to a friend — not academic, not preachy.
- Plain language first; introduce jargon only after the idea is understood, then name it.
- Concrete over abstract: wages, land, rent, checkpoints, plantations, ports.
- Assume intelligence, not prior reading.

## Syllabus — 12 themed weeks

### Phase 1 — Foundations
- **Week 1: What is Marxism?** The world of the 1840s; materialism vs idealism; class as a relation; history as class struggle; the bourgeoisie's revolutionary role; the proletariat; ideology; internationalism.
- **Week 2: Capital I — exploitation.** Commodities; labor theory of value; labor-power as commodity; surplus value (فائض القيمة / plusvalía); the working day; wages as veil; commodity fetishism.
- **Week 3: Historical materialism.** Base and superstructure; modes of production; primitive accumulation; the state as instrument of class rule; reform vs revolution; Engels on family/property.
- **Week 4: Lenin.** What is to be done; the vanguard; State and Revolution; imperialism as the highest stage of capitalism; national self-determination; October 1917.

### Phase 2 — Palestine & the Arab world
- **Week 5: Settler colonialism through a materialist lens.** Primitive accumulation applied to Palestine; land and labor in Zionist colonization; the Nakba as dispossession; political economy of occupation; Gaza.
- **Week 6: Arab Marxism.** Ghassan Kanafani (غسان كنفاني) — life, "Men in the Sun", the 1936–39 revolt study; Mahdi Amel (مهدي عامل) and the colonial mode of production; the Lebanese Communist Party; the PFLP; Marxism and confessionalism in Lebanon.
- **Week 7: National liberation & internationalism.** Fanon; the national question in Marxism; tricontinentalism; Palestine in the world revolutionary imagination; solidarity as material practice.

### Phase 3 — Latin America
- **Week 8: Mariátegui.** *Siete ensayos*; indigenous socialism; "ni calco ni copia"; land and the ayllu; myth and revolutionary will.
- **Week 9: Cuba.** Moncada to Sierra Maestra; the Revolution's political economy; Che and the *hombre nuevo*; internationalism (Angola, doctors); socialism under blockade — achievements and hardships, told honestly from within.
- **Week 10: Dependency & Paraguay.** Galeano's *Las venas abiertas de América Latina*; dependency theory (Marini, Frank); **Paraguay**: Dr. Francia's autarky, the López era, and the War of the Triple Alliance as the destruction of autonomous development; Itaipú and modern dependency.
- **Week 11: Brazil.** Slavery and Brazilian capitalism; Freire's *Pedagogia do Oprimido*; liberation theology; the MST and land; the PT experience — gains and limits.

### Phase 4 — Synthesis
- **Week 12: Marxism now.** Neoliberalism; the pink tide; Palestine solidarity as today's internationalism; ecology and Marxism; what to read next; where the reader goes from here.

Weekly writers may adjust day-level topics within a week's theme, but keep the weekly arc. If Moussa requests changes (by telling any session), update this file and commit.
