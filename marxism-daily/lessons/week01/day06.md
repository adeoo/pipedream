📜 **Where we are in history.** Before the *Manifesto*, in 1845–46, Marx and Engels filled hundreds of manuscript pages settling accounts with the German philosophy they were raised on. The book, *The German Ideology*, went unpublished for eighty years — they left it, Marx joked, "to the gnawing criticism of the mice." In it lies one of their most quietly explosive ideas.

**The lesson.** A puzzle should have been nagging you all week. If the exploited are the majority, and exploitation is as old as class society — why do the many so rarely rise against the few? Chains alone don't explain it. Something must make the chains feel natural, deserved, even holy.

That something is **ideology** — الأيديولوجيا. "The ideas of the ruling class are in every epoch the ruling ideas," wrote Marx. The class that owns the means of *material* production also controls the means of *mental* production — in his day the pulpit, the press, the schools; in ours the TV channels, the platforms, the sponsored curricula. So each ruling class presents its own interest as the universal interest, its own order as the natural order. Feudal lords had the Great Chain of Being: God placed the lord above the serf. Capital has its own catechism: the market rewards merit; the poor lack discipline; there is no alternative; الوضع هيك — "that's just how things are."

Ideology is not mainly lies told by liars. It is subtler: the horizon of the thinkable. It works best when it doesn't feel like ideas at all, just realism. And it localizes perfectly. In Lebanon, sectarianism does the work — convincing the Shia porter and the Maronite porter that they are essential rivals, so neither notices they share a boss, a landlord, and a collapsing currency. In Latin America, *el malinchismo* and the gospel of foreign investment teach that salvation only ever comes from outside and above.

But if consciousness is shaped by material life, it can be reshaped by material struggle. Marx noted that in revolutionary periods, ideas people held for generations dissolve in weeks. The fog is real — but it is fog, not stone. You felt this in October 2019, when a million Lebanese in the streets chanted against *all* the sects' leaders at once.

📖 **From the source.** *The German Ideology*:

> "The class which has the means of material production at its disposal, has control at the same time over the means of mental production... The ruling ideas are nothing more than the ideal expression of the dominant material relationships."

🤔 **Reflection.**
1. What belief did you absorb growing up — about sect, nation, money, or merit — that you can now trace to someone's material interest?
2. Who owns the media you and your family consume, in both your countries? Literally: look it up.
