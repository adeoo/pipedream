# Bike Frame Design & Building Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book/PDF: _The Paterek Manual for Bicycle Framebuilders_ by Tim Paterek (free supplemented PDF)](https://theframebuilders.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/The-Paterek-Manual.pdf)
  The canonical reference for lugged and fillet-brazed steel frame building.
  Use for: anything hands-on — mitering, brazing, alignment, frame prep.
- [Guide: "Getting started with Frame building" — The Framebuilders](https://theframebuilders.com/getting-started-with-framebuilding/)
  Curated starting-point roadmap from a framebuilding community site.
  Use for: orientation, what to read/buy/learn in what order.
- [Book: _Lugged Bicycle Frame Construction_ by Marc-André R. Chimonas](https://www.abebooks.com/Lugged-Bicycle-Frame-Construction-Manual-First/30514056338/bd)
  Written specifically for the first-time builder, low-tooling approach.
  Use for: planning the first build on a hobby budget.
- [Book: _Designing and Building Your Own Frameset_ by Richard P. Talbot](https://www.allbookstores.com/Designing-building-your-own-frameset/9780960241811)
  Classic amateur-focused text; covers design theory plus building your own jigs.
  Use for: the design-drawing stage; hand-tool-first mindset.
- [Site: Sheldon Brown — Articles about Bicycle Frames and Fitting](https://www.sheldonbrown.com/frames.html)
  The most trusted free reference for bicycle terminology and frame concepts.
  Use for: anatomy, glossary questions, frame materials, sizing basics.
- [Reference: Wikipedia — Bicycle frame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicycle_frame)
  Well-cited overview of the diamond frame and its variations.
  Use for: quick fact-checking of terms and history.
- [Chart: Surly Disc Trucker 700c — official geometry chart](https://surlybikes.com/products/disc-trucker-700c-hi-viz-black)
  Benchmark steel touring bike; the course's worked example of a geometry chart.
  Use for: real touring numbers when teaching geometry (lesson 0002 onward).
- [Tool: Geometry Geeks — community geometry database](https://geometrygeeks.bike/)
  Side-by-side geometry comparison of thousands of bikes; community-entered, so
  verify exact numbers against the manufacturer's own chart.
  Use for: browsing and comparing geometries across bike categories.
- [Guide: Sheldon Brown — Frame Sizing](https://www.sheldonbrown.com/frame-sizing.html)
  Trusted explanation of seat tube sizing conventions and effective top tube.
  Use for: sizing lessons (week 2) and the effective-top-tube gotcha.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Velocipede Salon — "The Path" frame forum](https://www.velocipedesalon.com/forum/f10/)
  Where many professional framebuilders hang out; high signal.
  Use for: showing work-in-progress designs, craft questions.
- [Bike Forums — Framebuilders section](https://www.bikeforums.net/framebuilders/)
  Friendly amateur-heavy forum. Use for: beginner questions without intimidation.
- [MTBR — Frame Building forum](https://www.mtbr.com/forums/frame-building.142/)
  Active builder forum. Use for: tooling and jig discussions.
