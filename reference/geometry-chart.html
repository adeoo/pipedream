Geometry Chart Cheat Sheet

How to read any frame's table of numbers. Taught in Lesson 2; example values are the 56cm Surly Disc Trucker, a benchmark steel touring bike.

The method: find your size column, then sort every row into one of two jobs — fit (serves the rider's body, grows with size) or behavior (serves the ride feel, often constant across sizes). Lengths are millimeters; angles are degrees from horizontal.

RowMeasuredJobTrucker 56cmFirst-look meaning
Seat tube length Bottom bracket → top of seat tube Fit560 mm The classic "frame size" — how tall the frame stands
Effective top tube Head tube → seat tube, measured level (not along a sloping tube) Fit575 mm How stretched-out you sit; the key fit length
Seat tube angle Seat tube's lean from horizontal Fit73° Places hips over the pedals; steeper = further forward
Head tube angle Head tube's (= steering axis') lean from horizontal Behavior72° Steering personality; slacker = calmer
Chainstay length Bottom bracket → rear dropout Behavior450 mm Long = stable, heel-room for panniers (race ≈ 410 mm)
Wheelbase Front axle → rear axle Behavior1051 mm Sum of all choices; long = steady ship (>1m = touring)
