# Mission: Bike Frame Design & Building

## Why
Adel wants to become a hobby craftsman who designs and builds steel city/touring
bike frames with his own hands. The first concrete milestone: design and build
one touring frame for himself, then build frames for friends and family as a
serious hobby.

## Success looks like
- Reads and critiques a frame geometry chart fluently (angles, lengths, trail)
- Produces a complete full-scale drawing of a touring frame designed for himself
- Chooses a tubeset, lugs and joining method for a touring frame and can say why
- Builds his first rideable brazed steel frame (once workshop access exists)

## Constraints
- Total beginner: rides bikes, but no wrenching or metalwork experience yet
- No workshop or tools yet — theory, design and paper work come first
- Prefers friendly, plain language; jargon must be introduced gently
- Learning through this workspace over multiple sessions

## Out of scope
- Carbon fiber and aluminum construction (steel is the hobby craftsman's path)
- Suspension design / mountain bike kinematics
- Running a framebuilding business (hobby craft only, for now)
